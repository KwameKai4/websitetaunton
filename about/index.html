About Us

Connecting Taunton's community with trusted local businesses

Our Mission

Taunton Somerset Directory was created to bridge the gap between local residents and the excellent businesses that serve our community. We understand how important it is to find reliable, trustworthy tradespeople and service providers when you need them most.

Our mission is simple: to make it easy for people in Taunton and surrounding Somerset areas to discover and connect with quality local businesses, while helping those businesses reach more customers in their community.

Why Choose Our Directory?

Local Focus

Exclusively featuring businesses serving Taunton and the surrounding Somerset area, ensuring you get local expertise.

Easy to Use

Trusted Listings
